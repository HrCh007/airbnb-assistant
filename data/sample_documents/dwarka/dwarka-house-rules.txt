FICTIONAL DEVELOPMENT DATA - Demo Property – Dwarka house rules.

# House rules
Smoking is not permitted anywhere in the apartment, including the balcony.
Small pets are allowed with prior approval from the host.
Visitors must sign in at the society gate with photo ID.
Quiet hours are from 11:00 PM to 7:00 AM as per society rules.
Parties and events are not permitted.

# Garbage disposal
Leave segregated garbage (wet and dry) outside the door by 8:00 AM; the society staff collect it daily.

# Security
The society gate is staffed 24 hours. Keep the apartment door locked and use the safety latch at night.
